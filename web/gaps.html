GEW, Iteration and Finance P&L

Revenue month by month, three ways (₹ Cr). Only months for which Finance has numbers

The Iteration sheet has two amounts for each student. Total Adjusted Collection (column K) is what the sheet counts as collected. Final Total Payable (w/o GST) is the fee the student has to pay. The table above shows the Iteration data, and how far it is from the GEW data and from Finance, with each of the two.

GEW data = our enrollment and collection sheets. Iteration data = the Iteration sheet (student by student, "Govt Offline Iteration till Aug YTD"), revenue worked out with the same rule as GEW, on the sheet's own "Total Adjusted Collection" (column K). Finance P&L = the "Revenue" line of finance's monthly P&L workbook (tab Govt exam - Offline). The Iteration data stops on 31 Aug, so part of GEW − Iteration is only timing (see "Why the two numbers differ"). Finance's student counts and collection (without GST) are close to GEW's (see the table below), so most of the difference in Finance's revenue seems to come from how finance works out its Revenue line. That rule is not written in any sheet and has to be confirmed with finance.

Finance's monthly P&L workbook is kept here only for comparison; it never changes any number in the portal.
